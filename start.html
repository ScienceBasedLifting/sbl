Reality check

Before you watch a single video, get these four things right. Everything else is noise.

  • Train each muscle 2× per week. Take most sets within a few reps of failure. That's the growth signal.
  • Eat 1.6–2.2 g protein per kg bodyweight. Hit that daily. The rest of your diet matters less than you think.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours. This is where you actually grow. No creator can fix bad sleep.
  • Stay consistent for months, not weeks. Swapping programs every 3 weeks is the #1 way to spin your wheels.

If you're not doing all four of these, no amount of content will help. Fix these first, then use the creators below to fine-tune.
