Learning paths

Learn in the right order.

Most lifters drown in advanced content before they have the basics locked in. These four stages give you a clear progression — master one before moving to the next.

Read this first

  • Train each muscle 2x per week with most sets within a few reps of failure.
  • Eat enough protein (1.6 to 2.2 g/kg body weight) and prioritize sleep.
  • Progress over months, not weeks. Consistency beats optimization.
  • Everything else on this site is nuance on top of these fundamentals.

The four stages

Where are you right now?

0

Pre-Foundations

You are brand new or under 6 months of consistent training.

  • Learn basic movement patterns (squat, hinge, press, pull)
  • Establish a sustainable gym habit (3-4 days/week)
  • Understand sets, reps, and progressive overload
  • Get protein and sleep roughly right
Beginner

Key creators: Jeff Nippard, Keenan Malloy, Scientific Snitch, JPG Coaching

 1

Foundations

6 to 18 months of training. You have a routine but want to understand why.

  • Programming literacy: volume, frequency, split selection
  • Exercise selection beyond defaults (why cable flies over pec deck?)
  • Nutrition fundamentals: cutting, bulking, maintenance
  • Recovery basics: deloads, sleep hygiene, stress management
Early Intermediate

Key creators: Jeff Nippard, Ryan Jewers, TNF, Ben Yanes, Mike Israetel, Scientific Snitch

 2

Intermediate Application

1.5 to 3 years. You understand the basics and want to optimize.

  • Biomechanics-driven exercise selection
  • Periodization and mesocycle design
  • Understanding resistance profiles and length-tension
  • Navigating contested topics (volume, partials, frequency)
Intermediate

Key creators: Joe Bennett, King Deltoids, Mike Israetel, Greg Nuckols, Eric Helms, Layne Norton

 3

Advanced Nuance

3+ years. You want the actual research and first-principles reasoning.

  • Individualized biomechanics (N-of-1 approach)
  • Reading and interpreting primary research
  • Advanced periodization for experienced lifters
  • Coaching others with evidence-based methods
Advanced

Key creators: Kassem Hanson, Milo Wolf, Keenan Malloy, Menno Henselmans, MuscleMechanicsLab, Juan Samudio
