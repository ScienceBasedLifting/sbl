About this site

How we pick who gets listed and why.

This is not a "top fitness influencers" list. It is a curated directory of creators who teach evidence-based lifting — evaluated on what they teach, how accurately they teach it, and how useful that teaching is for lifters at different stages.

Editorial stance

Consensus-first neutrality.

Where the hypertrophy literature converges — volume-responsiveness up to a personal ceiling, training near failure for hypertrophy, full ROM bias, protein around 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg, sleep and recovery primacy — we present it as settled. Where genuine evidence-based debate exists (exact volume prescriptions, lengthened partials vs full ROM, optimal frequency ceilings), we present both sides.

Bro-science without literature backing is not given equal weight. A creator who says "muscle confusion" is real gets evaluated differently than one who says "the optimal volume ceiling is debated." One is wrong. The other is honest about uncertainty.

Evaluation criteria

Six dimensions we evaluate.

Dimension 1

Scientific Depth

Does the creator cite research? Do they understand the difference between a single study and a meta-analysis? Do they update their views when new evidence emerges?

Dimension 2

Educational Usefulness

Does watching this creator actually make you better at training? Some creators are interesting but do not change what you do in the gym. We prioritize creators who produce actionable knowledge.

Dimension 3

Accuracy & Reliability

Does the creator overstate findings? Do they present single studies as settled science? Do they have documented accuracy issues? We flag concerns with explicit accuracy notes on profiles.

Dimension 4

Practical vs Theoretical Balance

Some creators teach theory beautifully but never show you what to do. Others show you what to do but never explain why. The best do both. We note where each creator falls on this spectrum.

Dimension 5

Audience Level Match

A creator who is excellent for advanced lifters might overwhelm beginners. We assign level ranges (beginner, intermediate, advanced) to every creator so you do not waste time on content above or below your stage.

Dimension 6

Content Integrity

Is the content primarily educational, or is it a sales funnel? We note when a creator's "where to start" is a paid product, and we distinguish between creators who sell programs (normal) and those whose content exists primarily to sell supplements or coaching.

Tier system

What the tiers mean.

Tier 1 — Primary educators

Creators who score highly across all six dimensions. They produce reliable, useful, evidence-based content that most lifters will benefit from. These are the creators the quiz routes people toward first.

Tier 2 — Valuable with context

Creators who are strong in their niche but have limitations: narrow scope, audience-level mismatch for beginners, or occasional accuracy concerns that require pairing with other sources. Still recommended, with notes.

Tier 3 — Niche or emerging

Creators with smaller audiences or newer track records who show promise. Listed with reduced visual prominence. We include them because the evidence-based space benefits from diverse voices, but we cannot yet assess long-term reliability.

Community — Peer-level creators

Smaller accounts run by knowledgeable lifters who contribute to the evidence-based conversation. Not formal educators, but worth following for specific takes.

Inspiration — Not educational

Competitive and pro-level lifters who train with evidence-informed methods. Separated from the educational directory because their training prescriptions do not transfer to most lifters. See the Inspiration page.

Transparency

Conflicts and corrections.

Conflict of interest

This site has no sponsorship deals, affiliate links, or paid placements. When a creator's best "where to start" content is a paid product, we disclose that inline. No creator has paid to be listed or has editorial input on their profile.

Corrections

If we got something wrong — a broken link, an inaccurate characterization, a missing accuracy note — we want to know. Creators and users can submit corrections. We review and update profiles when the evidence warrants it.

Last reviewed: May 2026

Why this exists

The evidence-based lifting space on social media is growing fast, and it is getting harder to separate genuine educators from content creators who use "science-based" as a marketing label. This directory exists because a lifter scrolling TikTok at 11pm should be able to find the five people actually worth learning from — and know why they are worth learning from.

We are opinionated about who we include, transparent about why, and open to being wrong. That is the whole editorial model.
